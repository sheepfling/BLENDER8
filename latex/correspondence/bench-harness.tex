\medskip\textbf{Independent driver permission}\par\small
MD20 ENABLE = MOTOR\_SUPPLY\_OK AND PGOOD AND RESET\_RELEASED AND BA-8 RUN\_PERMIT AND JAR\_OK AND JAR\_PERMIT AND PB0. STOP and the jar loop remove permission independently of firmware. Observe the PB0 request, PWM output and downstream ENABLE separately.
\normalsize\par\medskip
\textbf{Jar-loop interface / S2 and U\_IL}\par
{\small The S2 loop is closed only when the jug is fully seated and locked. Opening or a broken lead pulls JAR\_OK low and clears U\_IL within 1 ms. JAR\_PERMIT is its latched output: a new STOP assertion may set it only after 20 ms continuously closed. Reset/power loss clears it; opening dominates setting. STOP already held when closure qualifies does not set it. Contact transitions may bounce for 5 ms; every opening drops permission. This is a low-energy control circuit, not a series motor-current switch.}\par\medskip

The AVT10 uses the 3.3 V supply and analog return. Its package attaches thermally to the motor case. An open signal connection is pulled high by this board's diagnostic bias; that bias is not an intrinsic sensor output state.
