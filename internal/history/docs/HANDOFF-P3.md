# Handoff — six documents, publication P3

Hand Jordan only the six PDFs in `dist/recipient/`, or the single convenience binder containing
those same six sections. The first PDF is a boss email with retained acceptance and wiring
attachments. The fifth PDF collects the MD20 and AVT10 papers without merging their supplier
identities.

Do not hand over `source_inputs/`, owner notes, source ZIP, tests for the foundry, manifests,
historical board sheet, or identity-proof pages as component manuals. Clean copies in
`dist/recipient-clean/` are available for accessibility and source checking without changing the
interface contracts.

The current temperature sensor is the AVT10 powered linear voltage-output device. It is not a
thermocouple. A thermocouple alternative requires its own junction type, conditioning, reference
treatment and interface definition; none of those changes is made here.

B8 denotes the microcontroller. BA-8 denotes the button assembly.

The owner record explains fictional provenance. No provenance, teacher labels or grading commentary
belongs inside supplier pages. The email states business requirements and bench acceptance, not a
worked firmware solution. Generic manuals must remain usable for a different piece of equipment.
